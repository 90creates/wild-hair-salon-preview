FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when a stylist is available. Customers often get in the same day. Call (909) 798-1930 to check.

What are your hours?

Monday–Friday 9 AM–7 PM and Saturday 9 AM–4 PM. Closed Sunday.

Where are you located?

420 E State St in downtown Redlands, CA 92373.

Do you cut kids’ and men’s hair?

Yes. We cut women’s, men’s and kids’ hair.

Do you do vivid colors like green, pink or blue?

Yes. Vivid and fashion color is one of our favorite things to do.

Is the salon wheelchair accessible?

Yes. We have a wheelchair-accessible entrance, parking and restroom.
