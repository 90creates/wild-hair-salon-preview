FAQ

Vivid & Fashion Color: questions, answered

Have another question? Call (909) 798-1930.

Does my hair need to be bleached for vivid color?

Usually yes. Bright shades need a light base to show up true. On dark hair we lighten first, and very light hair sometimes needs little or no lightening.

How long does vivid color last?

It depends on the shade and how you care for it. Most fashion colors fade gradually over 4–8 weeks. Cool water and color-safe shampoo help a lot.

Can I do just a section instead of my whole head?

Yes. Underneath panels, a face-framing streak or tips are a great way to try vivid color.
