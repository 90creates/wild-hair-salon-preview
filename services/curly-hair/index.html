Curly Hair

How we approach it

Curly and wavy hair shrinks, springs and moves differently from straight hair, so the cut has to account for that. We look at how your curls fall before we take length off.

We’ll also suggest a simple routine and products so your curls look as good on day three as they do when you leave.

What’s included

  • Curly & wavy haircuts
  • Shaping to reduce bulk
  • Curl-friendly color
  • Defined curl styling
  • Routine & product advice

FAQ

Curly Hair: questions, answered

Have another question? Call (909) 798-1930.

Do you cut curly hair dry or wet?

It depends on your curl type and the look you want. We’ll talk it through at the start of your appointment.

Should I come in with my hair washed?

Come in with your curls styled the way you normally wear them so we can see your natural pattern.

Can I color curly hair?

Yes. We use a gentle approach and recommend care to keep curls healthy and defined.
