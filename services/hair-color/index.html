FAQ

Color, Highlights & Balayage: questions, answered

Have another question? Call (909) 798-1930.

How long does a highlight appointment take?

Usually two to three hours depending on your length, thickness and how much lightening you want. We’ll give you a time estimate when you book.

What’s the difference between highlights and balayage?

Highlights are placed in foils for even, defined brightness. Balayage is painted by hand for a softer, sun-kissed blend that grows out gently.

How often should I touch up my color?

Root color usually every 4–6 weeks. Highlights and balayage can often go 8–12 weeks or longer.

How much does color cost?

It depends on the service and your hair. Call (909) 798-1930 and we’ll give you a price range.
